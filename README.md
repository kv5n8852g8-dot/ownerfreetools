# OwnerFreeTools — free acquisition payback calculator

[Open the free calculator](https://ownerfreetools.onrender.com)

A free browser-based tool for evaluating small business acquisitions after accounting for the cost of replacing the seller’s work. No account or payment required.

## Example
A 10,000 purchase with 2,000 reserve, 1,000 monthly reported profit and 440 monthly replacement and extra costs gives 560 adjusted monthly profit. A 30% decline gives 260 downside profit, about 46.15 months of payback and a maximum purchase price of 7,360 for a 36-month target.
