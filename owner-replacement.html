How to estimate the owner's workload

Ask for a task log covering support, marketing, bookkeeping, supplier management and technical maintenance. Separate routine tasks from exceptional incidents. Obtain contractor quotes and include employer costs or agency fees where applicable. Do not subtract costs already included in reported profit twice.

Monthly cost = (weekly operations hours × rate + weekly specialist hours × rate) × 52 / 12 + additional monthly overhead.

For example, five hours at 25 plus two at 50 each week and 40 monthly overhead cost 1,015 per month. On 1,500 monthly profit, 485 remains before buyer taxes and financing.
